How
Fix the systems behind the scenes, as someone who's done the work.
Where
Most recently, healthcare, because health is the barrier that holds people back most.

Good medicine isn't enough on its own. Too often, systems built to deliver care slow it down: patients can't get access or answers, and clinicians spend their days on everything except patients. I work on what happens behind the scenes so care reaches the people who need it, and the people providing it can do their best work.

Work

  1. Now

    Independent Consultant

    I advise healthcare organizations on strategic growth and efficient operating structures, and I build simple products and tools that give clinicians more time for the deeply human work of delivering care. Most recently, I've been working with Dual Logic to embed HIPAA-compliant AI agents within healthcare organizations. I also continue to support Ackerly McBride Group in an advisory role.

  2. 2023 — 2026

    Co-Founder & Director of Strategy & Operations, Ackerly McBride Group

    Co-founded a concierge primary care practice and ran it like a product. I built the financial model before launch, then designed the service model, member experience, and workflows to match it. Grew the practice from 2 to 7 providers.

    I introduced AI into clinical and administrative work by doing discovery with clinicians and staff, piloting changes in small steps, and supporting adoption. Per-task cycle time dropped by half. I also gave a non-technical team an agile rhythm: a shared backlog, weekly prioritization, and retros.

  3. 2021 — 2024

    Senior Product Manager, 3Pillar Global

    Took two greenfield products from an undefined problem to launch with global teams of 10 and 15, including a pivot from B2B web to B2C mobile for a financial wellness startup. I owned vision, roadmap, and backlog, and turned ambiguous questions into decision frameworks executives could act on.

    I mentored five product managers; four were promoted or took on more scope. I also received the company's internal impact award twice.

  4. 2019 — 2021

    Associate Product Manager, Labcorp

    Improved an internal platform supporting $400M in annual revenue, balancing the needs of six delivery teams against the roadmap. Before that, I managed implementation for a 16-client portfolio, turning sales commitments into delivered service.
