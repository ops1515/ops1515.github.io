About

For more than ten years I've worked where product and operations meet, across healthcare and tech. I like walking into the chaos and figuring out the real problem, turning it into a prioritized roadmap, and building systems that teams actually use.

My toolkit is user discovery, roadmap prioritization, agile delivery, pricing and packaging, and AI-enabled workflow design. I work best alongside founders and executives, from defining the problem through launch and scale.

I have a Master of Public Health from Tulane, a psychology degree from Santa Clara, and a Lean Six Sigma Black Belt. Outside of work: a book club, Pilates, skiing, and traveling the world for gluten-free pastries.

Work

  1. Now

    Independent Consultant & Fractional CFO

    I advise healthcare organizations on strategic growth and efficient operating structures, and I build simple products and tools that give clinicians more time for the deeply human work of delivering care. I also stay on as fractional CFO and advisor to Ackerly McBride Group.

  2. 2023 — 2026

    Co-Founder & Director of Strategy & Operations, Ackerly McBride Group

    Co-founded a concierge primary care practice and ran it like a product. I built the financial model before launch, then designed the service model, member experience, and workflows to match it. Revenue came in 60% above forecast, and the practice grew from 2 physicians to 7 providers.

    I introduced AI into clinical and administrative work by doing discovery with clinicians and staff, piloting changes in small steps, and supporting adoption. Per-task cycle time dropped by half. I also gave a non-technical team an agile rhythm: a shared backlog, weekly prioritization, and retros.

  3. 2021 — 2024

    Senior Product Manager, 3Pillar Global

    Took two greenfield products from an undefined problem to launch with global teams of 10 and 15, including a pivot from B2B web to B2C mobile for a financial wellness startup. I owned vision, roadmap, and backlog, and turned ambiguous questions into decision frameworks executives could act on.

    I mentored five product managers; four were promoted or took on more scope. I also received the company's internal impact award twice.

  4. 2019 — 2021

    Associate Product Manager, Labcorp

    Improved an internal platform supporting $400M in annual revenue, balancing the needs of six delivery teams against the roadmap. Before that, I managed implementation for a 16-client portfolio, turning sales commitments into delivered service.
